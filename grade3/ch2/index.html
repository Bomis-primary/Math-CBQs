Grade 3 – Chapter 2: Addition up to 9999

Learning Outcomes Covered:
  • Recall and apply the properties of addition (Order, Identity of 0, Successor of +1).
  • Add 3-digit and 4-digit numbers without carrying over using visual place-value configurations.
  • Execute multi-digit addition with carrying over across tens, hundreds, or thousands columns.
  • Differentiate between addition problems that require carrying over and those that do not.
  • Formulate mathematical conclusions from real-world datasets using multi-step addition tracking.
