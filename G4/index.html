Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
• Read and write 5-digit and 6-digit numbers.
• Explain place and face value of digits in numbers.
• Formulate largest and smallest 5-digit/6-digit numbers from given digits.
• Arrange, sort, and compare large scale numbers with relational indicators.
• Evaluate calculations using strategic rounding to the nearest thousand.
• Convert and decipher traditional Roman and Hindu-Arabic numerals.
